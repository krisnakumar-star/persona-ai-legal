Privacy Policy

Persona AI

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Persona AI (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) handles information when you use the Persona AI mobile application. Persona AI provides personal finance organization features such as expense tracking, budgets, savings goals, recurring bills, financial summaries, and AI chat.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account information: information you provide to register or use your account, such as your name and email address, and authentication information needed to secure your account.
  • Financial information you enter: expenses, budget information, savings goals, recurring bills, and related details you choose to record in the app.
  • AI chat content: prompts and messages you choose to send to the in-app AI assistant, which may include information you type into those messages.
  • Support and account requests: information you include when contacting us for help or requesting account deletion.
  • Technical information: limited information may be processed by the app, hosting, database, or platform services to operate, protect, and troubleshoot the service. The precise logs and diagnostics depend on the services and configuration in use.

Please do not enter passwords for other services, payment-card numbers, bank login credentials, or other highly sensitive information into AI chat messages.

2. How we use information

Information is used to create and manage your account; save and display the financial records you enter; provide budgeting, savings, bill and expense features; respond to AI chat requests; send account-related emails such as password-reset messages where enabled; maintain service security; troubleshoot problems; and respond to support or deletion requests.

3. Storage and service providers

Persona AI uses MongoDB Atlas to store application data. The backend is hosted by Render. These providers process or store information as needed to operate the service, subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

When you use AI chat, the message content needed to generate a response is sent to Groq's AI API. Groq processes that content to provide the response under its applicable terms and privacy documentation. Do not send information to the AI assistant unless you are comfortable with it being processed for that purpose.

Persona AI may use Resend to deliver account-related emails, such as password-reset messages, where that feature is enabled. Information needed to deliver an email may be processed by that provider.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers described above to deliver app functionality, or disclosed when required by law, to protect the service and its users, or to address security or abuse issues. We do not intentionally make your financial records public.

5. Data retention and deletion

We retain account and app information for as long as needed to provide the service and for legitimate operational, security, or legal purposes. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing KRISHNAVISU12@GMAIL.COM from the email address associated with your account. Include “Persona AI account deletion request” in the subject. We may ask for reasonable information to verify the request. We will process verified requests and tell you if additional steps are needed.

Some limited records may need to be retained where required by law or for security and fraud prevention. Data held by third-party providers may also be subject to their own retention processes.

6. Security

We use service and technical measures intended to protect information. No internet-based service or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please keep your account credentials private and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

7. Children's privacy

Persona AI is a general personal finance organization app and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Your choices

You can choose what financial details to enter and what to include in AI chat messages. You may stop using the app and request account deletion using the contact details below.
